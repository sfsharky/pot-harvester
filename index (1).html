HOW IT WORKS
Setting up: tap the big DISTANCE TO NEXT number to set the spacing between pots, and POT PROGRESS to set how many pots are on the string. Choose your units (FT / M / YD, KTS / MPH) in the row above.

START / STOP / RESET: START begins the string and then turns into STOP, which asks you to confirm before it ends the string. RESET clears everything for a new string. The first time, tap Allow for location, sound and screen lock. Location works with no cell signal.

GRID mode: the app counts the distance from your last drop and alerts you when you reach the spacing. Drop on the alert and it counts down to the next one.

ACTUAL mode: for when pots don't always go over right on the mark. Tap the big TAG ACTUAL DROP button at the moment the gear goes overboard, and the spacing is measured from that real spot.

HOLD / BYPASS AREA: use it when you see ground you don't want to set on (reef, bad bottom). The deploy alert goes quiet but DISTANCE TO NEXT keeps counting as the boat moves, and it goes negative once you pass the spacing (-50 means 50 ft past the mark). You decide where the next pot goes: tap the button again (it now says RESUME - DROP HERE) at the spot you want. The horn sounds, that position is logged, and the full spacing starts again from there. Example with 100 ft spacing: hold at 60 ft and resume at 80 ft, and the next pot goes in at 80 ft. Resume at 150 ft, and it goes in at 150 ft, with the counter showing -50.

Compass: it uses GPS movement, so it only moves while you're moving. N-UP keeps north at the top and the arrow turns to your heading. H-UP keeps the arrow up and turns the ring so your heading is at the top.

DAY / NIGHT, DIMMER: NIGHT is the red night-vision screen. DIMMER darkens the whole screen further.

Full screen: tap ⛶ FULL at the top to hide the status bar and the bottom bar, which is easier on night vision. Tap ⛶ EXIT to bring them back. If it was on last time, one tap anywhere puts it back.

Sound and Bluetooth speakers: the horn, beeps and chimes play through whatever the phone is using for media. Pair a Bluetooth speaker (a JBL, for example) with the phone before you start, and the sounds come out of the speaker instead of the phone. Android plays sound in one place at a time, so don't count on the phone and the speaker sounding together. To hear it on deck and in the wheelhouse, use two speakers of the same make that link together (JBL calls this PartyBoost, other brands have their own name, so check the manual). Linked speakers play the same sound from a single phone connection. Some phones can also send sound to two Bluetooth speakers at once, so look in the phone's Bluetooth settings.

String Name: type a name for the string in the String Name box. It's used to name your export file.

End marker alert: a few seconds after the third-to-last pot goes over, the app says out loud “End marker reminder for gear following the current set.” Use the toggle at the top to turn it off. Use the menu to choose a voice or a chime, and tap TEST to hear it. The voice is a female voice when your phone has one, otherwise whatever English voice it has. If your phone has no speech voice installed, it uses the chime.

EXPORT (GPX): saves your pot positions as a waypoint file, saved under whatever you entered as the String Name, that you can open in your chart plotter or mapping software. After you've exported, RESET clears the string without any warning. If you reset before exporting, it asks first.
